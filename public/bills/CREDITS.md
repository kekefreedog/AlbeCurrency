# Banknote images

Fronts of the notes, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-17, then
cropped (1,000 LL only), rotated 90° and resized for the keyboard keys.

US law (18 U.S.C. § 504) allows color images of US currency when they are
one-sided and less than 75% or more than 150% of the real size, which these
key thumbnails are.
